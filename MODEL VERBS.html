Superhero Modal Verb Guide

Modal auxiliary verbs help main verbs express ability, certainty, advice, and permission. They never add "-s"!

CAN / COULD Ability & Polite Request:

"Leo can swim." (Ability)
"Could you pass the scissors?" (Polite)

MUST / SHOULD Obligation & Advice:

"You must wear a seatbelt." (Rule)
"You should eat vegetables." (Advice)

MAY / MIGHT Permission & Possibility:

"May I wash my hands?" (Permission)
"It might rain later." (Possibility)

WILL / WOULD Future & Preference:

"We will start at noon." (Certain)
"Would you like an apple?" (Polite offer)

Grade 5 Grammar Rule: Modals are directly followed by the base form of the main verb (e.g., "She must study", NEVER "She must studies" or "She must to study").
